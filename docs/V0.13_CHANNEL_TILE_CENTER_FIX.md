# v0.13 — exact channel TileWrapper center hit-zone

## Physical UI observation

On Orange TV Go the channel is not activated by clicking the logo or the details/text below the tile. Hover/focus reveals a Play overlay in the geometric center of the visual `TileWrapper`; the trusted tap must land in that exact center.

## Runtime contract

1. Resolve the desired `Channel-ChannelWrapper`.
2. Use `card.firstElementChild` as Orange `TileWrapper` — never `img[alt]` as the primary hit target.
3. Prime the tile with `pointerover`, `pointerenter`, `mouseover`, `mouseenter`, `mousemove` plus focus so Orange/React can reveal its Play overlay.
4. Compute the geometric center from the TileWrapper `getBoundingClientRect()`.
5. Return that exact coordinate for Android's trusted `MotionEvent`.
6. Keep DOM-events, React-onClick and HTMLElement.click fallbacks bound to TileWrapper.
7. Only after the tile activation wait for Orange player state (`background` / `expanded`).

## Regression test used locally

The v0.13 Playwright test reproduces the physical interaction:

- `mouseenter` on Polsat TileWrapper dynamically creates a Play button at `left:50%; top:50%`;
- `activateChannel(..., 0)` must trigger the hover state first;
- returned x/y must equal the TileWrapper center exactly;
- a trusted-equivalent click at those coordinates must hit the Play overlay and tune Polsat HD;
- background-player -> `IconPlayerScroll` -> expanded-player remains covered.

The user-provided saved Orange HTML files are not committed because they can contain session state/cookies.
